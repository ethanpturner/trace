## Context

Inherited-control recognition is a named evaluation metric in three documents —
`agent-design.md` sections 12 and 32, and `evaluation-plan.md` section 7 — and it is the
mechanism the ForgeFlow demo uses to suppress its clearest false positives.

`Control.inheritance_scope` is a free string (`data-model.md` section 18), and both
`data-model.md` open question 6 and `current-architecture.md` open question 5 leave the
representation open. There is a metric for a representation that does not exist.

`demo/forgeflow/forgeflow-scenario.md` section 12 lists ten existing controls, of which at
least six are inherited or shared: GitHub OAuth authentication, managed database
encryption, managed object-storage encryption, TLS, managed secrets, and the administrative
identity-aware proxy. That is enough of a corpus to design against rather than guess.

## Scope

Decide, working each of the six ForgeFlow inherited controls through the chosen
representation:

1. **What scope means.** `Control` already carries `provider_component_id` and the set of
   protected components and assets. Determine whether `inheritance_scope` earns its place
   given those two structured fields, or whether it is redundant.
2. **How an inherited control is distinguished from an assumed one.**
   `control_type: inherited` plus `implementation_status: claimed` is expressible today.
   Decide which combination represents "the platform provides this and the documentation
   says so" versus "the platform probably provides this and nothing says so." The second
   must never resolve to `absent` — that is DEC-009.
3. **What evidence an inheritance claim requires.** `forgeflow-scenario.md` section 14.2
   expects Trace to recognise an inherited control if documented **and** to ask for
   confirmation if inheritance is unclear. Those are two distinct outputs needing two
   distinct representations.
4. **Whether limitations are structured.** `Control.limitations` is `list[string]`. An
   inherited control's limits are exactly where its scope ends, so this may be where scope
   actually lives.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An entry is added to `docs/architecture/decision-log.md` answering all four questions.
- [ ] The entry works each of the six ForgeFlow inherited controls through the chosen
      representation and shows the result.
- [ ] The entry states which field combination produces "inherited and documented" versus
      "inheritance plausible but unconfirmed," and confirms the second never yields
      `absent` or `unmet`.
- [ ] `data-model.md` open question 6 and `current-architecture.md` open question 5 are
      marked resolved.
- [ ] If the decision changes `Control`'s field set, the entry says so explicitly and
      `data-model.md` section 18 is updated in the same pull request.

## Out of scope

- Enterprise control inheritance catalogs, deferred by `roadmap.md`.
- The mapping agent's inheritance reasoning, which is an M3 issue.
- The evidence threshold, which is DX-08.
