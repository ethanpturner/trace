## Context

The benchmark layout is specified twice, differently, and the ForgeFlow expected counts
disagree with the scenario document. Nothing can be authored until both are reconciled, and
whoever authors the first expected set fixes the convention for all 8 to 12 scenarios in
Stage 5.

**Layout conflict.** `docs/architecture/evaluation-plan.md` section 5 gives `README.md`,
`architecture.md`, `requirements.json`, `expected-context.yaml`, `expected-threats.yaml`,
`expected-findings.yaml`, `review-notes.md`. `demo/forgeflow/forgeflow-scenario.md`
section 25 gives seven `expected-*.yaml` files including `expected-control-mappings.yaml`,
`expected-questions.yaml`, `expected-documentation-gaps.yaml`, and
`expected-rejections.yaml`, plus `reviewer-notes.md` — a different filename for the same
file.

**Count conflict.** `demo/forgeflow/input/structured-system-input.yaml` declares
`findings: 3` and `questions: 5`. `forgeflow-scenario.md` section 19 lists **four**
findings (FND-001 through FND-004, noting FND-004 may reasonably merge into FND-002) and
section 20 lists **ten** candidate questions. `README.md` quotes the fixture counts as
authoritative. Three findings versus four is not a rounding difference; it is the
consolidation behaviour the workflow is graded on.

**Unresolved dependency.** DEC-010's first open question asks whether the per-scenario
`requirements.json` should reference catalog identifiers rather than restating requirement
text. Restating would fork the catalog that DEC-010 deliberately centralized.

## Scope

Decide and record:

1. **One layout**, with a documented schema for each expected-output file.
2. **`review-notes.md` versus `reviewer-notes.md`** — pick one and correct the other
   document.
3. **`requirements.json` resolves to catalog identifier references**, not restated text,
   closing DEC-010's first open question.
4. **Where scenarios live** — `benchmarks/` versus `demo/forgeflow/expected/` — and how the
   ForgeFlow demo relates to the benchmark suite. Both directories currently hold only
   `.gitkeep`.
5. **The ForgeFlow finding count**, recording whether FND-004 is a separate finding or a
   consolidation of FND-002, and resolving `forgeflow-scenario.md` open question 8 (whether
   prompt injection and automatic publishing are one finding or two), which determines it.
6. **Which five of the ten candidate questions are expected**, or amend the declared count.
