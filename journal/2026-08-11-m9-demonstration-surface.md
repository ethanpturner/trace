## What changed

  GET-only, on stdlib `http.server` with no web framework. Seven views, the differentiating one
  being the finding-lineage walk from a finding back through its critique, evidence assessment,

## What the numbers actually said

The reason the eval artifacts were worth building is that they were honest in both directions, and
writing the narrative forced me to read them rather than assume them:

- **Evidence validation is the load-bearing node.** Removing it raises the false-negative rate from
  0% to 100% on contradictory-docs and unsigned-webhooks — the pipeline stops producing the finding
  it otherwise reports. That is the one measured ablation result.
- **The critic and the context checkpoint moved no metric** on the three recorded scenarios. Stated
  as a null result rather than omitted, and bounded: an absence of measured effect on a small
  deterministic corpus is not evidence the components are idle. Measuring them needs scenarios
  authored to exercise a rejecting critic and an outcome-changing reviewer edit, which the corpus
  does not yet contain.
- **The forgeflow flagship recording misses its own truth set** (0 of 3 findings matched, 1
  spurious). It is an authored slice predating the full truth set. This is Trace's most prominent
  self-critical result and now appears in both the failure taxonomy and the ablation narrative
  rather than being smoothed over.

## Decisions and reasoning

- **stdlib `http.server`, no framework (DEC-078).** The view renders persisted objects; it needs no
  routing, templating, or ASGI server, and each would be a dependency on a project whose subject is
  architectural risk. The request-forgery mitigation is structural — a read-only surface has nothing
  to forge — rather than a token on a mutable endpoint, the same move DEC-005 makes for checkpoints.
- **Every eval artifact regenerates; none is hand-written.** comparison.md and ablation.md follow
  the scorecard's precedent: a generator over the committed feeds, a `--check` build script, and a
  CI drift check. This is what lets the limitations section and the narrative cite numbers without
  them rotting — the next scenario or the first live run updates them without a manual edit.
- **The static demo report is pinned to the existing hash.** Rather than introduce a drift-prone
  committed report, `test_demo_report_asset.py` ties `forgeflow-report.md` to `report-hash.txt`, so
  it fails loudly if it ever diverges from what the recording produces.
