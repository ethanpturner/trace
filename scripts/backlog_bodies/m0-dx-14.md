## Context

`docs/architecture/agent-design.md` section 7 lists "contradiction records or flagged
claims" as an output of the Context Extraction Agent. No object in
`docs/architecture/data-model.md` represents a contradiction. `ContextClaim` has a
`contradicted` status but no field pointing at the claim or evidence it conflicts with, so
a contradiction currently cannot be rendered to a reviewer or counted by the evaluation
harness.

This is not hypothetical. `demo/forgeflow/forgeflow-scenario.md` section 16 contains two
deliberate contradictions that Trace is required to surface rather than silently resolve,
and the fixture declares `contradictions: 2` as an expected output. The system is required
to do something it structurally cannot represent.

The two ForgeFlow cases are concrete enough to design against: source retention ("deleted
after analysis completes" in `product-overview.md` versus "30 days" in
`operations-guide.md` section 6), and AI output review ("externally visible AI-generated
output is reviewed before publication" in `security-overview.md` section 13 versus the
automatic-posting path in `operations-guide.md` section 9).

## Scope

### Options

1. **Two `ContextClaim` objects both marked `contradicted`, plus a blocking `Question`
   linking them** via `related_object_type` and `related_object_id`. No schema change, but
   the question can only point at one of the two claims.
2. **A `contradicts_id` or `conflicting_claim_ids` field on `ContextClaim`.** A data-model
   change, and the most direct representation.
3. **A first-class `Contradiction` object.** Cleanest, and expands the object count that
   `data-model.md` section 40 deliberately limits.
4. **Reuse `Critique` with `critique_type: contradictory_analysis`.** Section 40 defers
   `Critique` until the workflow operates, and it is the critic's object, not the
   extractor's.

### Decision criteria

- Can the reviewer see both sides at the context checkpoint, with the evidence for each?
- Can the evaluation harness count a contradiction deterministically against the fixture?
- Does the representation say explicitly that a contradiction is not itself a finding, and
  what it becomes instead?

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The chosen representation is demonstrated in prose against both ForgeFlow
      contradictions.
- [ ] If the decision changes `data-model.md`, the change is made in the same pull request
      and section 10 stays authoritative.
- [ ] The entry states explicitly that a contradiction does not become a finding
      (DEC-009), and what it becomes instead.
- [ ] The entry states what the reviewer sees at the context checkpoint.
- [ ] The entry states how the evaluation harness counts a contradiction, since the
      fixture declares an expected count.
- [ ] The entry notes that Trace must not silently choose the safer statement, per
      `forgeflow-scenario.md` section 16.1.

## Out of scope

- Implementing the representation, which is an M2 issue.
- Contradiction detection quality or any model-assisted comparison.
- Contradictions arising in later stages, in threats, mappings, or findings.
