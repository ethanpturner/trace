# The conditional-finding rule, and evidence validation learns to batch

Two deliveries closed the arc the flagship diagnosis opened, both by forked sessions, both
renumbering mid-flight as they raced each other into the log.

**DEC-133 (#589, PR #615) resolved the truth set's self-disagreement with a third shape**
neither side of the framing anticipated: a finding whose evidence is one side of a recorded
contradiction now names the resolution it depends on (`requires_resolution: Q-07`/`Q-08`), is
graded only in a run whose reviewer resolved the contradiction, and reports as
conditional-unreached — not missed — everywhere else, with the paired question carrying the
grade. A baseline, which has no reviewer, never reaches a conditional finding: naming the pair
means it chose a side silently, and it scores spurious — the scenario's own section-16 failure,
now enforced by the matcher. GW-13.2/13.3 and the finding expectations were both right about
different stages of the same assessment, and the contract now says so to the matcher.

**DEC-134 (#585, PR #616) made full evidence-assessment coverage a shape, not a hope.** The
call shape rides the pinned `workflow_version`: the constant moves to 0.2, every committed
recording pins 0.1 through a new per-entry registry field, and replay compatibility is
structural — the fifteen recordings, the flagship byte-for-byte replay, and the capture round
trip all replay under the shape they were recorded in. New runs batch the subjects in fixed
forties (claims and controls cluster under no threat, so DEC-024's per-threat shape was
engaged and declined), and under the batched shape an omitted subject is a retryable attempt
failure — the output-length excuse is gone at batch scale — while the single-call shape keeps
DEC-116's report-never-block honesty. Projected at corpus scale: eight calls, five-fold
output headroom, roughly 3–4x the single call's output spend with the DEC-105 cache split;
the measured figures ride #484 whenever its budget returns. The v1/v2 prompt pair is #331's
comparison subject, finally real.
