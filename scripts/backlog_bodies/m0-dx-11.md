## Context

Three documents assign severity to three different owners, and a fourth possibility is
implied by the data model.

- `docs/architecture/current-architecture.md` section 5.11 assigns preliminary severity
  during Finding Consolidation, a deterministic node.
- `docs/architecture/agent-design.md` section 17 assigns it to a Severity Support Agent.
- `agent-design.md` section 36 then declares that agent optional for the first
  demonstration and adds that "severity may initially be assigned by the reviewer."
- `Severity` includes `unassigned` (`data-model.md` section 4.5), suggesting it may simply
  be left unset.

`Finding.severity` is required. And shipping the Severity Support Agent would make a
seventh model-assisted component against a cap of six, which `CLAUDE.md` treats as a design
change requiring evidence that it improves results. Left undecided, the agent will be built
because it is fully specified, and the cap will be breached without an entry.

## Scope

### Options

1. **Exclude the agent.** The reviewer assigns severity at checkpoint 2; `Finding.severity`
   defaults to `unassigned`. Keeps the cap and one fewer thing to evaluate.
2. **Include it,** justified by evidence that it improves reviewer severity agreement. That
   evidence cannot exist before the agent does, so this option means accepting the cap
   breach on a promise.
3. **Deterministic severity heuristic** from asset impact fields and existing controls, no
   model. Design principle 7 favours it, though `data-model.md` section 4.5 warns against an
   overly complex severity algorithm before the core workflow is validated.

### Also decide

- Whether a reviewer may approve a finding whose severity is still `unassigned`.
- How a reviewer severity change is recorded. `ReviewDisposition` has no `change_severity`
  value, so it presumably arrives as `edit` with `prior_value` and `updated_value`. Confirm
  or add a value.

### Decision criteria

- Does the demo narrative depend on a proposed severity, or does the reviewer supply one?
- Does including it require a documented cap change, and is the evidence available?
- If deterministic, is the rule small and explainable, per section 4.5's warning?

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The entry states whether `unassigned` may survive approval.
- [ ] The entry states how a severity change is represented in `ReviewerDecision`.
- [ ] `current-architecture.md` section 5.11 and `agent-design.md` sections 17 and 36 are

- [ ] `README.md`'s pipeline diagram and agent table are made consistent with the answer.

## Out of scope

- Implementing the agent, if the decision is to ship one. That is a follow-up issue.
- CVSS or any quantitative scoring model. `agent-design.md` section 17 prohibits mechanical
  CVSS for architecture-only issues.
