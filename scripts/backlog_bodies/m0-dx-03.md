## Context

How evidence locations are represented is recorded as open in two documents
(`data-model.md` open question 3, `current-architecture.md` open question 4), and
`data-model.md` open question 2 asks the related question of whether evidence text is
duplicated into the store or read back from normalized files.

Underneath those sits a question nobody asked. `SourceDocument` carries both
`original_path` and `normalized_path`, and `EvidenceReference` carries both `quoted_text`
and `normalized_text`, but **nothing states whether `start_line` indexes the original or
the normalized document**. If normalization changes line counts, every evidence reference
in the system is off by an unknown offset, silently.

`EvidenceReference` is the object that the project's headline claim runs through — every
finding is designed to be walkable back to the sentence that produced it. Section 8 offers
five location fields and says only that "at least one usable source-location field should
be present when available." That is not enough to build a loader against, and it does not
address YAML or JSON at all: `demo/forgeflow/input/structured-system-input.yaml` has no
sections and no headings, and a line range points at a key whose meaning depends on its
parents.

## Scope

Decide and record:

1. **What normalization does concretely** — line endings, whitespace, Unicode form, front
   matter — and whether it may add or remove lines.
2. **Whether `start_line` and `end_line` index the original file or the normalized
   artifact**, and which of the two `quoted_text` is taken from. One unambiguous sentence.
3. **How a location in JSON and YAML is expressed.** A candidate is a JSON Pointer or
   dotted path carried in `metadata`, with `section_title` holding a readable equivalent.
   The decision must not add a field to `EvidenceReference` without recording that as a
   schema change; `data-model.md` section 8 is authoritative.
4. **What a chunk is and how `chunk_index` is assigned** — by heading, by size, or by
   structural node. Note that the demo corpus is not consistent about heading depth:
   `architecture-overview.md` is 734 lines with 28 second-level headings, while
   `sample-repository-notes.md` uses first-level headings for the same purpose.
5. **Whether nested headings produce nested or flattened `section_title` values**, and
   what happens to a source with no headings at all.
6. **Whether `page_number` stays unused** until PDF ingestion arrives.

Compare at least two candidate representations against the real corpus in
`demo/forgeflow/input/`, not against a hypothetical document.

## Out of scope

- Implementing segmentation and indexing, which are M1 issues.
- Vector embedding or semantic chunking, deferred by `current-architecture.md` section 17.
- PDF page handling.
