## Context

This is the dial between the false positives Trace exists to avoid and the false negatives
that would make it useless, and it is undefined.

`AssessmentConfiguration.evidence_threshold` is a **required** field whose only stated
value is the example string `direct-or-confirmed`. No vocabulary is defined anywhere. The
same question is recorded as open in three places at once: `data-model.md` open question
15, `current-architecture.md` open question 7, and DEC-009's own open questions.

Separately and more sharply, **nothing defines what evidence justifies `unmet`**.
`ControlMapping.satisfaction_status` admits it, and `agent-design.md` section 13 requires
the Mapping Validation node to "prevent unverified from silently becoming unmet" — a guard
with nothing to check against.

Until this is settled, the DEC-009 boundary is enforced only by prompt wording, and
`docs/product/design-principles.md` section 7 states plainly that a rule whose violation
makes the system behave incorrectly does not belong in a prompt.

## Scope

Produce a decision-log entry defining:

1. **The permitted values of `evidence_threshold`** and which is the default. The default
   must be safe under the secure-defaults principle.
2. **A complete truth table** from (`satisfaction_status`, `validation_status`,
   `confidence`) to one of: provisional Finding, DocumentationGap, Question, no output.
   No cell may yield a Finding from absence of documentation alone.
3. **What justifies `unmet`** — positive evidence that a control is absent or inadequate,
   ranked by the evidence hierarchy in `agent-design.md` section 14. State plainly that no
   quantity of silence reaches the threshold. This is DEC-009 expressed as a rule a
   deterministic validator can apply.
4. **What justifies `satisfied` versus `partially_satisfied`.** `agent-design.md`
   section 12 forbids marking a control implemented "without evidence or confirmation," so
   both need an evidence floor, not only `unmet`.
5. **Whether `unverified` may ever produce a low-confidence candidate finding**, and under
   exactly what additional conditions. `data-model.md` section 19 permits it; nothing says
   when.
6. **What an "explicit low-confidence justification" is**, since `Finding` minimum
   validation rules accept it in place of evidence.
7. **Where the threshold is enforced** — at consolidation, at approval, or both.

### Decision criteria

- Is every rule expressible in deterministic code, per design principle 7?
- Do the four intentional non-findings in `demo/forgeflow/forgeflow-scenario.md`
  section 14 get suppressed by it, and do the five genuine weaknesses in section 13
  survive it?
- Does the entry make clear that a high proportion of `unverified` mappings is the
  expected outcome and not a defect? `requirements/README.md` states that requirements are
  phrased so absence of evidence resolves to `unverified`, never `unmet`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] An entry is added to `docs/architecture/decision-log.md` in the established format.
- [ ] The entry names a closed set of `evidence_threshold` values and one default.
- [ ] The entry contains the complete truth table, with no cell in which absence of
      documentation alone yields a Finding.
- [ ] The entry defines the evidence conditions for each `satisfaction_status` value as a
      rule a deterministic validator can check.
- [ ] The entry works through at least three ForgeFlow cases and states the expected
      status for each: the webhook authenticity ambiguity (section 15.1), the webhook
      replay weakness (section 13.1), and the absent local password policy (section 14.1).
- [ ] `data-model.md` section 6 is updated to reference the vocabulary.
- [ ] Data-model open question 15, `current-architecture.md` open question 7, and DEC-009's
      first two open questions are marked resolved.
- [ ] The entry states that a high proportion of `unverified` is not a defect and must not
      be treated as one in evaluation.

## Out of scope

- Severity, which is DX-11.
- `common_false_positives` enforcement, which is DX-09.
- Implementing the rule, which is an M3 and M4 issue.
- Changing requirement catalog content.
